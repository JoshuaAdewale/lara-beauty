Our story

From a Lagos kitchen to a London shelf

Lara made her first batch of glow oil in a Lagos kitchen, for her mother, whose skin had turned dry and dull after years of harsh soaps. Friends asked. Then friends of friends. Then a queue formed outside the gate.

When Lara moved to the UK, the orders followed her — first to friends in London, then to women across Britain who could not find anything like it on the high street. Rather than choose between the two, we kept both.

Everything is still made in small batches in Lagos, from shea sourced through the same women’s co-operative in Kwara. A share of every batch is flown to our London store room, so UK orders ship domestically instead of waiting weeks at customs. Same formulas, same hands, two front doors.

What we believe

Good skincare should be short, honest and affordable enough to use every day. We would rather make five things properly than fifty things adequately.

How we make it

Everything is produced in small batches at our Lagos studio. Shea comes from a women-led co-operative in Kwara, and our black soap is made to a traditional Ghanaian recipe using plantain ash and cocoa pod. Nothing is diluted with cheap filler oils, and we do not test on animals.

Two front doors

We hold stock in two places. Nigerian orders are packed and posted in Lagos. UK and European orders ship from our London store room, so nothing sits in customs and nobody pays an import surprise at the door. Prices are set for each market rather than converted, which is why you can shop in Naira or Euros and see a fair figure either way.

Who it is for

Every formula is tested on skin from fair to deep and on women from their twenties to their seventies. If a product does not suit your skin, send it back within 30 days — even if you have opened it.
