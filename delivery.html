Nigerian orders are packed and dispatched from our Lagos studio. UK, Irish and European orders ship from our London store room, so they arrive as domestic post with no customs delay and no import charge on the doorstep.

Both dispatch within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday. Orders placed on Sunday or a public holiday are sent the next working day.

Pay on delivery

Available in Lagos and Abuja. Choose Pay on delivery at checkout and settle with the courier in cash or by transfer when your parcel arrives. UK orders are prepaid at checkout.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves us. You can also check the status any time on our order tracking page.

Returns — our 30-day promise

If a product does not agree with your skin, send it back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. This applies even if the item has been opened and used — we would rather you found the right product than kept the wrong one.

To start a return, email info.larabeautyatelier@gmail.com with your order reference. We will send return instructions the same working day. Refunds are processed within five working days of the parcel reaching us.

Damaged or incorrect items

Send a photo to info.larabeautyatelier@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery and we will replace the item at no cost to you.
